Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? Find quick answers about lessons, schedules, licenses, and reschedule rules below.

How many lessons do I need to pass the road test?
+
The number of lessons depends on individual experience. Complete beginners typically require between 10 to 15 hours of professional on-road lessons combined with private practice to fully prepare for the state examination.
Do you offer pick-up and drop-off services?
+
Yes! We offer convenient pick-up and drop-off services directly from your home, school, or workplace for all behind-the-wheel driving instruction sessions.
Can I choose between manual and automatic cars?
+
Absolutely. We maintain a diverse training fleet that includes both automatic and manual transmission vehicles, allowing you to select your preferred configuration.
What is your cancellation and reschedule policy?
+
We require at least 24 hours notice to reschedule or cancel a driving lesson. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice may incur a late cancellation fee.
Is the official driving handbook included in the courses?
+
Yes, all our students receive a digital copy of our comprehensive driving handbook. You can also download it for free directly from the handbook section on our landing page.
